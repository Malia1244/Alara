Alara prd
Overview:
Alara is an AI-based study software designed to help students track, organize, and manage their learning journey. Alara continuously collects and remembers the information students provide daily, allowing it to build a deeper understanding of each student's learning progress. Students can provide information through various forms, including notes, worksheets, homework, or personal explanations written in their own words. Using this stored data, Alara generates personalized quizzes after each daily input, ensuring that students remember what they learned that day as well as previous lessons. Over time, Alara becomes increasingly personalized by analyzing each student's learning patterns, identifying their strengths, and recognizing topics where they need additional support. This allows Alara to provide more effective study recommendations, helping students strengthen their understanding, improve information retention, and develop healthy study habits for the future.  

Problem:
Many students rely on ineffective study habits that make it harder to remember information over the long term. Surveys show that 99% of students admit to cramming, and nearly half of all students regularly rely on it as their primary study method. Cramming is often ineffective because the brain can only process a limited amount of new information in a short period of time. Think of it like pouring a giant bucket of water into a tiny cup, most of the water spills out and is lost. Additionally, 10% of students choose to stay awake all night studying instead of sleeping. Research has shown that students who get at least eight hours of sleep the night before an exam generally perform better than those who do not. Despite this, many students continue to rely on last-minute studying because they lack a consistent and personalized way to review what they learn each day. Alara addresses this problem by encouraging students to review and reinforce their learning every day. After each daily learning session, Alara generates personalized quizzes based on both recent and previously learned material, helping students strengthen long-term memory and build lasting understanding. By studying for just 10–20 minutes each day, students can develop consistent study habits and reduce the need for hours of cramming the night before an exam.

Goals:
Helps students build consistent study habits.
Improves long term memory through regular review.
Reduces the amount of cramming the night before an exam.
Increases student’s confidence with  classroom material.
Encourages greater classroom engagement by helping students better understand what they are learning.

Alara uses quiz results to determine which topics need additional review and which topics the student already understands. 
Alara identifies specific topics or questions the student struggles with 
Alara tracks quiz scores and saves the results to the student profile
